# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals doing focused, self-directed work at a desktop or laptop. The timer lives on-screen (often in a second window or a corner) while they work in another app. Their context is a single-player focus session: start a block, work heads-down, take a break, repeat. They come back day after day, so the app is a habit surface, not a one-off tool.

The job to be done: protect a block of attention, feel progress accumulate, and want to start the next block.

## Product Purpose

A Pomodoro focus timer that makes sustained deep work feel rewarding. It runs work/break cycles, tracks sessions completed, and turns consistency into visible momentum (streaks, daily counts, progress you can feel). Success is the user finishing more focus blocks than they would have alone, and returning tomorrow because the app made today feel good.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, encouraging, focused. Three words: **motivating, sharp, alive.** The voice cheers real progress without being saccharine. It celebrates completed blocks and streaks the way a good training app does: earned praise, momentum you can see, a small hit of satisfaction on completion. Confident and quick, never noisy while the user is trying to concentrate.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard.** No card grids, no hero-metric templates, no cool-gray Inter-everywhere sameness. Progress and stats must be expressed with committed color and real hierarchy, not KPI tiles.
- **Sterile corporate.** Not cold, not humorless, not navy-and-gray enterprise. This is a personal tool with personality.
- **Cluttered / busy.** During an active session the screen must be quiet. Stats, history, and controls should not compete with the running timer. Depth lives one layer back, not all at once.

Gamification is welcome (streaks, rewards, momentum), but it must read as earned and sharp, not toy-like or infantilizing.

## Design Principles

- **Momentum is the product.** The core loop is start → focus → complete → feel it. Completion and streaks deserve the app's strongest moments of color, motion, and reward.
- **Focus first, stats second.** The running timer owns the screen during a session. Everything else recedes until the block ends. Never make the user parse a dashboard mid-focus.
- **Earned energy, not decoration.** Bold color and motion appear at meaningful moments (a block completing, a streak advancing), not as ambient noise. Loud where it counts, quiet everywhere else.
- **Practice what you preach.** A tool that sells focus must not distract. Restraint during work is the product working.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast throughout, including the energetic accent colors against their backgrounds. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: the celebratory motion (progress fills, completion effects, streak animations) must degrade to instant or minimal state changes when reduced motion is requested, without losing the information they convey.
